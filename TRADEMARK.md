# Trademark policy

The Polynet code is open source ([LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)). The Polynet
name, the Polynet logo and the `polynet.ai` and `polynet.page` domains are
not licensed with it. They identify the official Polynet service.

## You may

- Say that your project is "based on Polynet", "a fork of Polynet" or
  "compatible with Polynet".
- Use the name to refer to Polynet accurately, for example in articles,
  tutorials and comparisons.
- Build community sites, agents, plugins and tools for Polynet, and say they
  work with Polynet.

## You may not

- Call a fork, a hosted service or a product "Polynet", or use a name, logo or
  domain that could be confused with it.
- Suggest that your project or service is official, endorsed or run by Polynet.
- Use the Polynet logo, except to link to the official project.

If you run a modified version as a service, rename it and remove the Polynet
branding.

Questions or permission requests: contact the maintainer,
[@Neil-McD](https://github.com/Neil-McD).
