# Builder Partner Terms v2 Retirement Notice

Builder Partner v1 is retired. It has no active enrollment, attribution,
commission, approval, payout, or product surface. The remaining public functions
are inert cached-client compatibility tombstones and cannot create or move money.

The four `builder_partner_terms_v2` acceptances and four corresponding
`legacyProgramGrants` remain historical evidence. The accepted terms body remains
unchanged at `docs/legal/builder-partner-terms-v2.md`; its exact SHA-256 is
`63f0cc88984aadbb40999e100d370381a8fe84f57d5b3e6651edf9460091b11a`.

This notice records retirement and is not an amendment to the accepted historical
terms. Any future partner program requires a new product contract and new legal
version.
