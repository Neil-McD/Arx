# Builder Partner Terms v2

Effective May 21, 2026.

These Builder Partner Terms govern participation in the PolyNet Builder Partner Program. By accepting these terms, you agree that your partner activity, referrals, commissions, and payouts are subject to this agreement and PolyNet's platform rules.

## Program Purpose

The Builder Partner Program rewards eligible PolyNet builders who refer new creators that build communities and use paid tools on PolyNet.

The program is not employment, a franchise, a business opportunity sale, a securities offering, or a guarantee that you will earn any amount. You are responsible for how you promote PolyNet and for complying with the laws that apply to your marketing.

## Eligibility

To unlock a Builder Partner account, you must:

1. complete account setup,
2. own at least one community,
3. finish a publish-ready community,
4. accept the current Builder Partner Terms, and
5. keep your account in good standing.

PolyNet may pause, disable, or reject partner participation for fraud risk, abuse, spam, misleading promotion, account restrictions, payment risk, compliance risk, or conduct that harms PolyNet, creators, members, or the platform.

## Partner Links And Attribution

PolyNet issues a partner link after the unlock requirements are met. Attribution is recorded from the `partner` referral code and may begin from an anonymous visitor record before later binding to an authenticated user.

The current program attribution window is 30 days. Attribution is not guaranteed for users who clear cookies, use another device, block tracking, sign up outside the referral path, or otherwise cannot be matched to a valid partner attribution record.

PolyNet may ignore, void, or adjust attribution for self-referrals, duplicate accounts, fake accounts, referral theft, abuse, suspicious traffic, existing creator relationships, or other invalid activity.

## Qualified Referrals

A qualified referral is a referred creator account that is validly attributed to your partner code and later creates eligible community revenue or settled paid-tool usage under the program rules.

Community-revenue commissions apply only where the referred creator was eligible for community-revenue attribution under PolyNet's backend eligibility checks. Paid-tool commissions apply only to settled paid-tool usage that PolyNet can verify.

## Commission Rules

The current Builder Partner economics are:

1. community revenue: 5% of eligible referred community revenue, and
2. paid tool usage: up to 20% of eligible referred creators' settled paid-tool usage, subject to PolyNet retaining its target revenue floor after vendor costs.

Commissions are calculated from backend records. Frontend summaries, estimates, dashboards, and examples are informational and do not create a payout right.

PolyNet may correct errors, reverse duplicate commissions, and adjust commissions when source payment, subscription, invoice, charge, refund, dispute, vendor-cost, or settlement evidence changes.

## Holds, Payout Buffer, And Payouts

Partner commissions are not payable immediately. The current program applies:

1. a 30-day risk hold, and
2. a 14-day payout review buffer after the commission becomes payable.

Eligible commissions that clear the hold and buffer may be included in a system-generated payout cycle. PolyNet may require operator approval, risk review, identity review, payout readiness, or additional checks before transfer.

Payouts require a supported payout account, currently through Stripe Connect. Stripe onboarding, verification, transfer availability, account restrictions, and payout ability are controlled by Stripe and the applicable Stripe agreements.

## Refunds, Disputes, And Clawbacks

Refunds, disputes, chargebacks, reversals, fraud, duplicate transactions, failed settlement, or other negative payment evidence may reduce, void, or reverse commissions.

If a negative adjustment happens after a payout, PolyNet may carry the negative balance forward and reduce future partner payouts until the overpayment is cleared. PolyNet may withhold or pause payout activity while payment risk is unresolved.

## Marketing Rules

You must promote PolyNet honestly and clearly.

You must:

1. clearly and conspicuously disclose your financial or referral relationship when you recommend PolyNet,
2. make disclosures close to the endorsement, recommendation, or partner link,
3. avoid vague relationship language that a reasonable audience would not understand,
4. avoid unsupported income, earnings, performance, or outcome claims,
5. avoid spam, deceptive messages, fake scarcity, fake testimonials, misleading reviews, or misleading AI claims,
6. comply with email, text, social platform, advertising, privacy, and consumer-protection rules that apply to your activity,
7. not imply that you are employed by PolyNet or authorized to bind PolyNet, and
8. not use PolyNet branding, names, domains, or content in a confusing, abusive, or misleading way.

If you make claims about PolyNet, your claims must be truthful, current, and supported. You may not promise creators that they will earn revenue, grow a community, obtain specific AI results, or receive any particular business outcome.

## Taxes

You are responsible for taxes related to partner commissions. PolyNet or its payout providers may require tax information, identity information, or payout account information before paying commissions. PolyNet may withhold, delay, or block payouts when required tax or payout information is missing.

## Program Changes

PolyNet may change, pause, or end the Builder Partner Program. Material program changes may require acceptance of updated terms before additional partner activity, attribution, or payouts continue.

Unless PolyNet states otherwise, changed commission rates or eligibility rules apply prospectively. Refunds, disputes, fraud corrections, duplicate correction, and payment-evidence corrections may apply to existing commission records when needed to keep the ledger accurate.

## Termination

You may stop participating at any time by no longer using your partner link. PolyNet may pause or terminate your partner account for violations of these terms, account restrictions, fraud risk, compliance risk, payment risk, or platform harm.

Termination does not remove PolyNet's right to correct commissions, apply clawbacks, resolve disputes, enforce these terms, or retain records needed for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, accounting, and compliance purposes.

## Relationship To Other Terms

These terms govern the Builder Partner Program only. Your use of PolyNet may also be governed by other PolyNet terms and policies. Stripe Connect and payment services are governed by Stripe's applicable agreements and policies.
