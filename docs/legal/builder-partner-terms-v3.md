# Builder Partner Terms v3

Effective July 20, 2026.

These Builder Partner Terms govern participation in the Polynet Builder Partner Program. By accepting them, you agree that partner activity, attribution, commissions, adjustments, and payouts are subject to this agreement and Polynet's platform rules.

## Purpose And Eligibility

The program rewards eligible Polynet builders who refer new creators whose communities generate eligible settled revenue. It does not pay commissions for tool usage. It is not employment, a franchise, a securities offering, or a guarantee of earnings.

To participate, you must complete account setup, own a community, finish a publish-ready community, accept the current terms, and keep your account in good standing. Polynet may pause or disable participation for fraud, abuse, spam, misleading promotion, account restrictions, payment risk, compliance risk, or platform harm.

## Attribution And Earning Window

Polynet records an eligible referral when a valid partner link is followed and the referral can be bound to an authenticated creator under backend eligibility checks. The click-to-bind attribution window is 30 days. Once validly bound under the current economic policy, the referral can earn eligible commissions for 12 months from the first authenticated bind. Repeated visits do not restart that 12-month period. A referral bound under an earlier economic policy does not automatically restart or convert into a v3 attribution.

Attribution is not guaranteed when a user clears storage, changes devices, blocks tracking, signs up outside the referral path, or cannot otherwise be matched. Polynet may ignore, expire, void, or adjust self-referrals, duplicate accounts, fake accounts, referral theft, suspicious traffic, existing creator relationships, and other invalid activity.

## Commission Economics

Under economic policy `builder_partner_economics_v3`, the target commission is 4% of eligible settled community revenue generated by a referred creator's communities. Eligible revenue is the amount actually settled after discounts and excluding tax. Refunds, disputes, chargebacks, reversals, and payment corrections reduce or reverse that basis.

The commission is paid from Polynet's platform fee and never reduces the community builder's payout. A commission cannot exceed the platform fee Polynet retained on the source transaction. Therefore the effective commission may be less than 4% when the applicable platform fee is lower than 4%.

No new paid-tool or tool-usage commission accrues under this version. Dashboard estimates and examples are informational; only the backend ledger and verified provider evidence create a payout obligation.

Every new commission records its economic policy version, eligible-revenue basis, policy rate, effective rate, and source platform-fee rate. Existing commission and payout records created under earlier accepted terms remain governed by their recorded terms and economics; this version does not retroactively rewrite them.

## Holds, Adjustments, And Payouts

New commissions enter a 30-day risk hold and then a 14-day payout review buffer. Eligible commissions may then enter a system-generated payout cycle, subject to payout readiness, risk review, identity review, operator approval, and provider availability.

Refunds, disputes, chargebacks, reversals, fraud, duplicate transactions, failed settlement, or corrected provider evidence may reduce, void, or reverse commissions. If an adjustment occurs after payout, Polynet may carry the negative balance forward against future eligible commissions.

Payouts currently require a supported Stripe Connect account. Stripe controls onboarding, verification, account restrictions, transfer availability, and payout ability under its agreements.

## Marketing, Taxes, And Program Changes

You must disclose your financial relationship clearly and near any endorsement or partner link. You must not use spam, deceptive claims, fake scarcity, unsupported income claims, misleading reviews, misleading AI claims, or confusing use of Polynet branding. You may not imply employment by Polynet or authority to bind Polynet.

You are responsible for taxes on partner commissions. Polynet or its providers may require tax, identity, or payout information before payment.

Polynet may change, pause, or end the program prospectively. Material changes may require acceptance of new terms before new attribution or commission accrual continues. Termination does not remove Polynet's right to correct the ledger, apply adjustments, resolve disputes, enforce these terms, or retain legally necessary records.

These terms govern only the Builder Partner Program. Other Polynet terms and provider agreements may also apply.
