# Privacy & AI Data Policy v1 Supersession Notice

`privacy_ai_data_policy_v1` is an immutable historical legal document. Its
source remains at `docs/legal/privacy-ai-data-policy-v1.md` without amendment.

Privacy v2 adds what Polynet shares with outside AI apps a person connects
through Polynet's MCP connector (Claude, ChatGPT and others), what those apps
can and cannot do, how to disconnect, deletion and export requests by email,
and support@polynet.ai as the contact. Existing v1 acceptance records remain
historical evidence and still satisfy current acceptance, so members who
accepted v1 are not asked again; v2 is shown at /privacy. New acceptance
writes require `privacy_ai_data_policy_v2`.
