# Polynet Privacy & AI Data Policy v1

Effective May 21, 2026.

This Privacy & AI Data Policy explains how Polynet collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use Polynet, including accounts, communities, AI features, paid communities, paid tools, and connected services.

## Information We Collect

Polynet may collect information you provide directly, information created through your use of the service, information from connected providers, and limited technical information needed to operate the product.

This may include:

1. account information, such as name, email, authentication identifiers, profile details, handle, avatar, and account settings;
2. community information, such as community names, descriptions, settings, resources, memberships, roles, join requests, posts, messages, and moderation activity;
3. AI and tool activity, such as prompts, messages, model or provider selections, tool inputs, tool outputs, usage records, runtime evidence, voice input metadata, and settlement evidence;
4. payment and commerce information, such as checkout status, subscription status, entitlement state, Stripe Connect readiness, payout status, refunds, disputes, chargeback evidence, platform fees, partner commissions, and related audit records;
5. connected provider information, such as whether a provider account is connected, provider account metadata, token status, key status, or connection health;
6. device, log, cookie, local storage, and analytics information, such as pages viewed, routes, approximate timestamps, browser or device metadata, session state, error logs, security logs, and product usage events; and
7. support, safety, fraud-prevention, and compliance information you or third parties provide.

Polynet does not intentionally collect full payment card numbers. Payment card processing is handled by Stripe or another supported payment provider.

## How We Use Information

Polynet uses information to:

1. create, authenticate, and secure accounts;
2. operate communities, memberships, roles, access, resources, and community AI features;
3. provide AI, search, voice, tool, workspace, and connected-app functionality;
4. process billing, entitlements, platform fees, refunds, disputes, partner commissions, and payout readiness;
5. personalize and improve product experiences;
6. detect abuse, fraud, security issues, policy violations, and payment risk;
7. debug, monitor, analyze, and improve reliability and performance;
8. provide support and respond to requests;
9. comply with legal, tax, accounting, payment-provider, and safety obligations; and
10. enforce Polynet terms, policies, and platform rules.

## AI Features And Provider Processing

When you use AI or tool features, Polynet may send relevant inputs, context, files, prompts, messages, resources, metadata, or tool instructions to AI model providers, search providers, transcription providers, compute providers, connected apps, or other subprocessors needed to complete the requested action.

The exact provider may depend on your settings, connected accounts, selected model, selected tool, community configuration, product availability, or fallback behavior.

AI and tool providers may process submitted information according to their own terms, privacy policies, retention settings, and product behavior. Do not submit sensitive information unless you intend it to be processed for the feature you are using and have the right to submit it.

Polynet does not intentionally use private user content to train general-purpose foundation models unless Polynet clearly states otherwise or asks for separate permission. Third-party AI providers may process AI inputs and outputs according to their own terms and settings.

## Community Memory And Shared Context

Shared community activity, posts, messages, resources, configuration, and other community materials may be used to power that community's intelligence, memory, retrieval, summaries, and AI context.

Private workspace, profile, account, provider, and personal AI context should remain private unless you intentionally share it, use a feature that sends it to a provider, or take an action that makes it part of a community or shared surface.

Community builders and authorized community roles may be able to view or manage certain community materials, members, resources, settings, and activity according to Polynet's role and access controls.

## Payments, Payouts, And Commerce Data

Polynet uses Stripe, Stripe Connect, or other supported payment providers for payments, subscriptions, community revenue, payout readiness, and related payment operations.

Polynet may store payment status, checkout identifiers, subscription identifiers, entitlement state, refund and dispute state, charge or invoice evidence, platform fee records, partner commission records, payout readiness, and audit metadata needed to operate access, billing, fraud prevention, risk review, accounting, support, and compliance.

Stripe and other payment providers may collect and process payment, identity, tax, bank, and payout information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## Connected Services

If you connect a third-party service, Polynet may store and use connection status, provider identifiers, tokens, keys, scopes, account metadata, and tool results needed to provide the integration.

You can disconnect or rotate connected providers where the product supports it. Some records may remain where needed for security, audit, billing, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or product integrity.

## Sharing Information

Polynet may share information:

1. with service providers and subprocessors that help operate Polynet;
2. with AI, tool, compute, search, transcription, infrastructure, analytics, error-monitoring, support, and security providers;
3. with payment providers for payments, subscriptions, payouts, fraud prevention, disputes, taxes, and compliance;
4. with community builders, admins, moderators, and members according to community roles, settings, and product surfaces;
5. with connected apps or providers when you authorize or use an integration;
6. when required by law, legal process, tax, accounting, payment-provider, or compliance obligations;
7. to enforce terms, investigate abuse, prevent harm, or protect Polynet, users, communities, providers, or the public; and
8. in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or transfer of assets.

Polynet does not sell full payment card data. Polynet does not intentionally sell private user messages. If Polynet adds advertising, marketing, or data-sharing practices that require additional notice or consent, Polynet will provide the required notice or controls.

## Retention

Polynet keeps information for as long as needed to provide the service, operate communities, maintain access and billing truth, resolve disputes, enforce policies, prevent fraud, support users, satisfy legal or accounting obligations, and maintain security and audit records.

Deleting content or an account may not immediately remove backups, logs, payment records, audit records, dispute records, legal records, security records, or information already processed by third-party providers.

## Security

Polynet uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No system is perfectly secure, and Polynet cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed, disclosed, altered, or lost.

You are responsible for securing your account, devices, connected providers, API keys, tokens, and credentials.

## User Choices

Depending on product availability and legal requirements, you may be able to:

1. update profile and account information;
2. manage connected providers;
3. leave communities or adjust community participation;
4. request deletion or export of certain information;
5. ask questions about privacy or data handling; and
6. opt out of certain optional communications or controls where Polynet provides them.

Some requests may be limited by security, identity verification, legal obligations, payment records, fraud prevention, dispute handling, community integrity, or technical constraints.

## Children

Polynet is not intended for children under 13. If Polynet learns that it has collected personal information from a child under 13 without required consent, it will take appropriate steps to delete or disable that information.

## International Use

Polynet may process information in the United States and other locations where Polynet, its providers, or subprocessors operate. Data protection laws may differ from those in your location.

## Changes

Polynet may update this policy. Material changes may require notice, acknowledgment, or updated acceptance where appropriate. The effective date and version identify the policy in effect.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support or contact channel made available in the Polynet product.
