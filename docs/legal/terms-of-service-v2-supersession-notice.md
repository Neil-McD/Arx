# Terms of Service v2 Supersession Notice

`terms_of_service_v2` is an immutable historical legal document. Its source
remains at `docs/legal/terms-of-service-v2.md` without amendment.

Terms v3 changes the commercial boundary for newly created communities and the
interaction between Builder funding and community lifecycle. Existing v1 and v2
acceptance records remain historical evidence, but do not satisfy current v3
acceptance. New acceptance writes require `terms_of_service_v3`.
