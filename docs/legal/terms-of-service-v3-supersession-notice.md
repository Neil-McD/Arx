# Terms of Service v3 Supersession Notice

`terms_of_service_v3` is an immutable historical legal document. Its source
remains at `docs/legal/terms-of-service-v3.md` without amendment.

Terms v4 makes communities free to create and run, and moves Polynet-hosted AI
to a per-community Agent plan ($79 USD per month, including up to $20 of raw
model cost per billing cycle). Without a current Agent plan, hosted AI pauses
and the community keeps operating. Existing Builder subscriptions continue as
the community's Agent plan. Existing v1, v2 and v3 acceptance records remain
historical evidence, but do not satisfy current v4 acceptance. New acceptance
writes require `terms_of_service_v4`.
