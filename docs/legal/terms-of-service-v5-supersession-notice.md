remains at `docs/legal/terms-of-service-v5.md` without amendment.

month with $3 of included AI use; Chief of Staff, $100 a month with $20 and the
proactive features), adds optional $30 Usage Boosts on Chief of Staff, and says
existing $79 subscriptions continue at $79 with Chief of Staff features.

current acceptance, so members who accepted them are not asked again; v6 is
shown at /terms. v1-v3 acceptances do not. New acceptance writes require
`terms_of_service_v6`.
