# Course cover prompts: Polynet community

Three covers, one series. Each course is a step closer in: orbit → a city → its centre.

## Ground truth they follow

- **Shape:** the course cover is 16:9 (Knowledge shelf card, about 280×156 on screen) and also shows as a tiny thumbnail beside the lesson list. One clear subject, centred, nothing important in the top-right corner (the drag handle sits there). No text in the image.
- **Polynet's own look:** the community banner is Earth at night from orbit, city lights, faint network arcs. The logo is a hub with five linked nodes, a diagonal gradient from blue #2f80f7 to violet #8246f5.
- **The app:** calm, Linear-style graphite (#08090a to #0f1011), colour only on small things. Purple (#a78bfa) means the AI, nowhere else.
- **Neil's taste:** premium, restrained, "timeless futurism". Not "AI slop": no glowing brains, robots, sparkles, holograms or neon.
- **Export:** 1600×900 JPEG, under about 800 KB (the cover upload limit is around 1.2 MB once encoded). Upload in the app: open the course, click the cover, **Add a cover**.

## Round 2 (Nov 23): brighter and more hopeful

Neil on round 1: right concept, but too bland, too dark, not optimistic. It doesn't need to look like a photo; it needs to look good. So: illustration instead of night photography, sunrise light, and the brand blue and violet as real colour.

## Shared style (paste at the start of every prompt)

> Optimistic retro-futurist poster illustration, 16:9. Soft painterly gradients and clean shapes, like a modern NASA "visions of the future" travel poster drawn by a premium editorial illustrator. Sunrise palette: deep blue sky melting into warm peach, rose and gold near the horizon, with Polynet's blue (#2f80f7) and violet (#8246f5) as the accent colours. Bright, luminous, airy, lots of light, subtle grain. Hopeful, warm and inviting. One clear subject in the centre, calm empty space in the top-right corner. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI, no people, no robots, no neon, no holograms, no dark or gloomy areas.

## 1. Start here

> [shared style] Earth seen from orbit at sunrise. A bright golden sun just cresting the curved horizon, the sky glowing peach and rose into clear blue, oceans catching the first light. On the edge between night and day, one warm point of light where a journey begins, with a few soft city lights still twinkling on the night side. Big, open, hopeful: the first morning.

## 2. Build your community, A to Z

> [shared style] A small, lovely settlement seen from above at golden hour, on soft rolling land with rivers catching the light. A bright central plaza and five smaller glowing neighbourhoods arranged in a ring around it. Golden paths of light are being drawn from the centre out to each neighbourhood; three are finished, two are half-drawn with a small bright spark at their tip, like construction in progress. Warm, constructive, full of possibility.

## 3. Your community's AI

> [shared style] The same settlement at dusk, now complete: five warm, glowing neighbourhoods all connected by golden paths to the central plaza. The central plaza glows a soft, friendly violet (#8246f5 into #a78bfa), and gentle violet light flows out along every path to the neighbourhoods. The sky above is a smooth blue-to-violet gradient with the first stars. Calm, alive, quietly intelligent, welcoming.

Why the shapes: a centre with five around it is the Polynet logo's shape (hub and five nodes), told as a place people build rather than drawn as an icon. Violet appears only in course 3, because violet is the AI's colour.

## If a result goes wrong

- **Too dark or flat:** add "brighter, higher key, more sunrise colour in the sky".
- **Too sci-fi or glowy:** add "soft and painterly, no glow halos, no bloom".
- **The logo appears literally:** add "do not draw a logo or icon; organic city lights only".
- **Too busy at thumbnail size:** add "fewer lights, larger empty areas, one focal point".
- **Colour creeping in:** repeat "only warm white and pale blue lights" (and for course 3, "violet only at the centre point").

## Plan B: flat, graphic series

If photographs feel too heavy next to the app:

> Minimal flat vector illustration, 16:9, near-black graphite background, a few thin precise white lines and small circles, lots of empty space, Swiss modernist poster style, no text.
> 1. A single small circle with a thin horizon arc beneath it.
> 2. Six small circles in a ring, some joined by thin lines, some not yet.
> 3. The same ring fully joined; the centre circle filled with soft violet #a78bfa.
