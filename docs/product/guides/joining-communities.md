# Joining Communities

## Goal

Help members understand how community access works.

## Mental Model

A community may be public to view, but gated to join or use. Access can depend on membership, approval, payment, or a grant.

## Rules

1. Published does not mean free access.
2. Unlisted does not mean impossible to join.
3. The funnel is the main public entry path.
4. Real access begins when the backend grants membership or entitlement.
5. If access is removed or blocked, the user must not be able to read or act
   through that community context.
