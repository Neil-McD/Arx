# Member Quickstart

## Goal

Join communities, take part, use shared knowledge, and get help from each
community's agent.

## Core Steps

1. Sign in.
2. Open a community from Discover or a shared link, and join through its join
   page.
3. Read and post in the community's channels.
4. Use its resources and events.
5. Open the community's Agent page (the Agent tab on mobile) to talk with the
   community agent privately, or @mention the agent in a channel to get a reply
   everyone there can see.
6. Ask for help with a concrete task.

## Member Mental Model

A community gives you access to people, shared knowledge, and its agent around
a domain. Each community has one agent. It belongs to the community and helps
you inside your own permissions there. Your private conversation with it stays
yours.

## Rules

1. You can only use a community, and its agent, when you have access.
2. Your Agent-page conversation is private to you in that community. It does
   not become shared community memory and does not carry into other
   communities.
3. An @mention reply is posted in the channel where everyone with access can
   read it.
4. Community resources and accepted memory are shared community knowledge.
5. Paid or gated communities may require approval, purchase, or membership.
