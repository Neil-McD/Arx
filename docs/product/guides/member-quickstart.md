# Member Quickstart

## Goal

Join communities, participate, use shared knowledge, and work with your
personal agent under an authorized community context.

## Core Steps

1. Sign in.
2. Join or open a community.
3. Use community chat and resources.
4. Open the bottom-right personal-agent dock.
5. Ask the agent for help with a concrete task.
6. When you are on a community page, verify that the dock shows that
   community as the active context.

## Member Mental Model

Communities give you access to people, shared knowledge, and agent capability
around a domain. The personal-agent dock is the member-owned surface for using
that context.

## Rules

1. You can only use community context when you have access.
2. Community context affects subsequent turns and does not rewrite stored history.
3. Community resources and accepted memory are shared community knowledge, not
   private member-agent conversation history.
4. Paid or gated communities may require approval, purchase, or membership.
5. The community-owned agent is separate from your personal agent.
