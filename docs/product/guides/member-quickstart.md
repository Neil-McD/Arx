# Member Quickstart

## Goal

Join communities, participate, and use your personal agent under authorized community context.

## Core Steps

1. Sign in.
2. Discover a community or open its shared funnel.
3. Complete the join, approval, or paid-access gate.
4. Enter the community and use its chat, resources, and voice surfaces.
5. Open the bottom-right personal agent.
6. From a community page, ask the agent for help with a concrete task.
7. Navigate to Discover or a generic platform page when the next turn should not use community context.

## Member Mental Model

Communities give you access to people, shared knowledge, and agent capability around a domain. Your personal agent stays yours while the current authorized community page determines the context for subsequent turns.

## Rules

1. You can only use community context when you have access.
2. Changing pages affects subsequent agent turns, not prior turns.
3. Community resources are shared knowledge, not private memory.
4. Paid or gated communities may require approval, purchase, or membership.
