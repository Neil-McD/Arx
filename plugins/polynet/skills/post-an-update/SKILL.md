# Post an update

Turn what the person told you into one post, and send it only when they say so.

1. Read `/communities/<slug>/rules.md`. The host's house rules come first: if they say where a kind of post goes, or that it is not wanted, follow that.
2. List `channels/` and pick the one channel the post belongs in. If two fit, ask.
3. Read that channel's recent posts. If the same thing was said in the last few days, reply in that thread instead of starting a new post.
4. Write the post:
   - Their words and their facts. Add nothing they did not tell you.
   - The point in the first sentence. Under 120 words unless they asked for more.
   - No greeting, no sign-off, no hashtags, no emoji they would not use.
   - Mention a person only if the post is for them.
5. Show them the post and the channel. Send it with `polynet_write` only after they say yes.

After sending, give them the path of the post. It is marked "via AI" for everyone.

If the server refuses (too long, too many links, a repeat, a limit), tell the person what it said and fix the post. Do not reword it to slip past the check.
