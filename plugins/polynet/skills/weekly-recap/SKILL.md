# Weekly recap

For hosts. One post that tells a member who was away what mattered this week.

1. Read `/communities/<slug>/rules.md` and `community.md`.
2. Read `brief.md` if it is listed. Then read the week's posts in each channel, and the threads with the most replies.
3. Pick at most five things. Prefer: a question that got a useful answer, a win, a decision, something members made. Leave out arguments, anything from a pod or a private place, and anything about one person that they might not want repeated.
4. Write it:
   - A title line with the dates.
   - One line per item: what happened and who, with the path to the thread.
   - "Coming up" with next week's events, if there are any.
   - Under 250 words. No opening paragraph, no closing paragraph.
5. Show the host the draft and the channel it would go in. Post it only after they say yes.

If little happened, say so to the host and suggest skipping the week. A recap of nothing teaches members to ignore recaps.
